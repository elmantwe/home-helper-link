This skill uses your own Gemini API key. Your Gemini usage is billed to your own Google account, under that key's quota and billing settings. There is no shared key.

المهارة دي بتستخدم مفتاح Gemini بتاعك أنت. الاستخدام بيتحسب على حساب جوجل بتاعك.

  1. Create a key in Google AI Studio. A dedicated key, restricted to the Gemini API, is recommended.
  2. Paste it below and press Link.

The key goes directly from this page to Amazon's account-linking address. This page's server never receives it. Amazon stores it as your account-linking token and sends it only with your own requests to the skill, which uses it to call Gemini for you. To change it, say "change my Gemini key". To remove it, disable the skill in the Alexa app.

This page can only be opened from the Alexa app's Link Account button.
